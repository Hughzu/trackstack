🎯 Evaluation Guide

As you explore these directions, consider:

  • The "1-Second Glance": Which direction lets you comprehend your status fastest?
  • The "Recents Access": Which layout makes logging a repeat meal most effortless?
  • The "Chat Persistence": Which approach keeps the chat bar accessible without being intrusive?
  • The "Visual Hierarchy": Which size/spacing feels most "Trade Republic" to you?
  • The "Brunch Flow": Which direction supports rapid successive logging best?

📝 Design Direction Notes

  • Direction 1 (Classic): Pure vertical stack. Maximum number size. Recents as horizontal scroll. Best for "Glance + Scroll to Log" flow.
  • Direction 2 (Progress): Adds progress bars for visual "budget consumption" feedback. Grid-based Recents. Best for users who like visual progress indicators.
  • Direction 3 (Cards): Compact side-by-side cards. More space for Recents list. Shows calorie values in Recents. Best for users who want more context before tapping.
  • Direction 4 (Ultra): Absolutely minimal. Largest numbers (96px). Fewest elements. Best for pure "Glance" users who rarely scroll.
